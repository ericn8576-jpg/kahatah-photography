The Visionary Behind The Brand

"Quality is Not an Accident; It Is Our Signature."

I am Kahatah Finnest, CEO and Lead Creative Director of Kahatah Studios. Born out of an undeniable passion for visual poetry and authentic storytelling, Kahatah Studios was established in Karatina Town, Nyeri County to redefine the photography experience in Central Kenya and beyond.

To me, every person who steps into our Karatina studio or invites us to document their wedding ceremony carries a unique, irreplaceable story. We blend high-end studio lighting, cinematic color palettes, and friendly artistic coaching so you never feel uncomfortable or posed.

Our motto — "The Home of Quality Assurance" — is an active promise. We treat every client like family, ensuring every shot is backed by our full creative dedication and guaranteed satisfaction.
