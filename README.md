## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. Curated Portfolio & Interactive Lightbox
- **Categorized Galleries**: Filter dynamically between *All Works*, *Weddings & Love*, *Editorial & Fashion*, *Portraits & Studio*, *Couples & Engagements*, and *Events & Galas*.
- **Native `<dialog>` Lightbox**: Fullscreen-compatible responsive modal with zoom control (1.6x), keyboard navigation (Left/Right/Esc), and swipe support.
- **Camera EXIF Details**: Displays camera body (*Leica M11, Hasselblad X2D, Sony A7R V*), focal length lens, shutter speed, aperture, and ISO.
- **Quick-Book Action**: *"Book This Style"* shortcut inside each photo card and lightbox that pre-selects the package and jumps to the reservation form.

### 2. Curated Packages & Investment in Kenyan Shillings (KES)
- **Multi-Currency Converter**: Choose between **KES (KSh)**, **USD ($)**, **EUR (€)**, and **GBP (£)** with automatic rate conversion.
- **4 Curated Packages**:
  - **The Executive Portrait** (KSh 4,500): Individual & executive studio portraits in Karatina, 15 master retouched files, quality assurance guaranteed.
  - **The Signature Love** (KSh 12,000): Couples & engagements, 2.5 hours golden hour session, 40 retouched images, drone angles, framed A3 archival print.
  - **The Grand Royal Wedding** (KSh 45,000 - *Most Popular*): Full-day coverage across Nyeri County, 2 master shooters led by CEO Kahatah, 350+ deliverables, and a luxury mounted photo album.
  - **Commercial & Corporate Gala** (KSh 25,000): Half-day commercial coverage, 100+ deliverables, 48-hour turnaround, 4K video teaser reel.
- **Bespoke Add-Ons Calculator**: Real-time addition of 4K drone photography (+KSh 5,000), luxury heirloom albums (+KSh 8,000), extra hours (+KSh 3,000), and 48-hour rush delivery (+KSh 4,000).

### 3. Smart Booking Studio ("Book a Session")
- **Live Reservation Flow**: Date picker, lighting time slot (*Morning Light, Midday Studio, Sunset Golden Hour, Twilight*), shoot location (*Karatina Studio, Mt. Kenya Outdoors, Wedding Venue, Nationwide*), contact info, and vision notes.
- **Sticky Real-Time Summary Sidebar**: Live calculation and preview of the estimated total in KES.
- **Instant Booking Confirmation**:
  - Generates a unique reference code (e.g. `KHT-2026-7842`).
  - **Direct WhatsApp Chat**: Opens a pre-formatted WhatsApp chat directly with CEO Kahatah Finnest at `0115824621`.
  - **Calendar Sync (.ics)**: Downloads a standard iCalendar event file for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook.
  - **Studio Docket / Inquiries Portal**: Automatically saves reservations to browser `localStorage` for offline review.

### 4. High-End Editorial Aesthetics
- **Obsidian Dark & Alabaster Light Modes**: Persistent theme toggle with smooth luxury transitions and gold foil accents.
- **Pure Modern Web Stack**: Zero heavy framework overhead, zero build dependencies. Native modern HTML5, CSS custom properties, and vanilla JS.
